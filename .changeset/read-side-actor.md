Range filters on read-model tables, the actor on the read side, and an access log that records failures. A table's `where`, in `findMany`, `findOne`, `count`, `update` and `delete`, takes per field a value, matched by equality as before, or operators: `gt`, `gte`, `lt` and `lte` on strings, numbers and dates, `ne` (which keeps rows without a value; `ne: null` keeps only those with one) and `in` on those and booleans; `json` fields match by equality only. An operand left `undefined` adds no condition, but a field whose operators have none is refused rather than matching every row. The types refuse an operator a field has no use for, and the runtime an unknown one with `ConfigurationError`. `Where`, `FieldFilter`, `RangeFilter` and `SetFilter` are exported. A `json` field can no longer be a read model's primary key. `exportPersonalData` pages each read model's rows by primary key instead of by offset, so a projection writing meanwhile cannot make a row skip or repeat. Every access record gains `outcome` (`"ok" | "failed"`, `AccessOutcome`): a query, an export or a dead-letter call that fails after reading is recorded as `failed`, since its error may quote what it read, and a failed export's `detail` is `null`. A query that takes `client.raw` is recorded with `unattributed: true`, which a query's record now always carries. A retry or discard of a dead letter is recorded once it acted, with its outcome, rather than before. Query `repository` and `handler` receive `actor`, the client's (`as`), `undefined` without one; `actor` is a reserved port name in a read model, and a query with a repository cannot name a payload field `client`, `table` or `actor`. Spans of projections, policies, processes and scheduled runs carry `bounda.actor`, and queries (`bounda.query <Type>`) and the client operations `forget`, `restrict`, `unrestrict`, `exportPersonalData`, `rebuildReadModel` and `deadLetters.*` (`bounda.operation <operation>`) get spans of their own with it. `ProcessResumed` after a dead-letter retry carries the actor of whoever retried.
