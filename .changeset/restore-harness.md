The restore watch also watches the newest event each instance read, or committed as a forgetting, at its position: a copy that lost it is found at the next pass, even one taken in the five seconds before the generation moved, and an instance in the `web` role finds it as well. The dispatcher checks at the start of every pass, and a pass that finds a restore reconciles the forget ledger before anything is delivered or claimed, so a scheduled command of someone the copy brought back never runs. An app without a forget ledger logs an error when it finds a restore. `restoreContract` in `@bounda-dev/core/adapter/testing` is the end-to-end restore an adapter has to pass: persons write, a backup is taken, some are forgotten, the master key rotates, and the copy is restored with the app stopped and, when the engine can, running, also under an instance in the `web` role, without a forget ledger through `app.restored()`, with a forgetting the ledger recorded whose commit never happened, and with a strict ledger that cannot be read at the restored start; `expectForgotten` checks every person forgotten after the copy, and the app goes on. It runs on node:sqlite, libSQL, PostgreSQL and the Durable Object. The new guide "Backups and restores" has the steps for each engine and what is guaranteed.
