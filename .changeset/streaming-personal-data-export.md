Export a person's data however long their history. `streamPersonalData(personId)` on every `BoundaClient` hands out the export a part at a time as it reads it, a page of records at most held at once: the header first (everything but the records, with `position`, the head of the stream when it began, and the read models whose rows follow), then each event, scheduled command, dead letter, subscriber failure, answer and read-model row, section by section. It resolves once the header is read, so a refusal rejects before anything is handed out. `personalDataExportJson(parts)` writes the parts as a `ReadableStream` of the same JSON document `exportPersonalData` answers, reading a part only when the stream is read; `exportPersonalData` is now built on the stream, and gains `position`. The export holds one view of the person: its events stop at `position`, every other store is read in pages by a key no write changes, and an export during which the person was forgotten, restricted, lifted or redacted rejects with `ConcurrencyError` once it has read everything. The access log records a streamed export when its reading ends, with how many records of each kind it handed out by then, as `ok`, `failed` or the new `cancelled` (`AccessOutcome`); the export's `detail` is never `null` any more. On Cloudflare `connect(stub).streamPersonalData` reads the object's export over RPC as a stream of its parts, stopped when the Worker stops reading. React Router adds `personalDataExportResponse(client, personId, init?)`, a streamed, uncached response a loader returns, also from `@bounda-dev/react-router/app`. `bounda export` becomes `bounda personal-data export <person> [--output <file>]`, which streams to standard output at the pace it is read, or to a file readable by its owner alone, moved into place only once whole. Adapters: `listByPerson` on the scheduler, the dead letter store and the idempotency store takes `{ person, after?, limit? }` and pages by key (`ListByPersonArgs`), and `personalDataExportContract` in `@bounda-dev/core/adapter/testing` checks a long export on an adapter's database.
