Every call of an operation the access log records now leaves a record however it ends (article 5(2)). A call refused before it opens anything is recorded with the new outcome `refused` (`ReadOutcome`, exported beside `AccessOutcome`): a query of a read model with personal fields whose database is not served (`READ_MODEL_RESTORING`), whose payload or `includeRestricted` does not validate; an export or `recipientsOf` without a person id; a dead letter or subscriber failure call with an `includeRestricted` that is no basis; a `retry` or `discard` of a letter already settled or not there; a read of stored letters or failures, still sealed, that fails. A refused record names no one, since nothing was opened, but the person an export or `recipientsOf` was asked for, and keeps the basis it named when it is one. An export refused with `READ_MODEL_RESTORING`, recorded `failed` before, is now `refused`. A `deadLetters.get` or `subscriberFailures.get` that finds nothing is recorded `ok`, naming no one, as a query that matches no row is. Query, export, dead letter, subscriber failure and `recipientsOf` records are typed with `ReadOutcome`; the others keep `AccessOutcome`.
