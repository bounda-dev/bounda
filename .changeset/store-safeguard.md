A store may be kept in another jurisdiction than `storage` under a safeguard of chapter V of the GDPR. Every adapter, `masterKey`, `defineKeyWrapper`, `defineTenantKeyStore` and `defineForgetLedger` take `safeguard` beside `location`, one of the new `StoreSafeguard`s (`STORE_SAFEGUARDS`): `adequacy` (article 45), `standard-contractual-clauses`, `code-of-conduct`, `certification` (46(2)) or `binding-corporate-rules` (47), the article of each in `TRANSFER_ARTICLES`. `DataLocationOptions`, `StoreDefinitionOptions` and `MasterKeyOptions` are now types that take a `safeguard` only beside a `location`, and `placementOf` from `@bounda-dev/core/adapter` carries both into an adapter's definition. The app refuses to start, with `ConfigurationError`, on a read model, tenant key store, forget ledger or key wrapper kept in another location without a safeguard, on a safeguard where `storage` keeps the app's data, on `storage` itself or under a `storage` that declares no location, on a safeguard without a location, and on a `derogation`, which article 49 allows for occasional transfers alone. The record of processing gives every holder and record a `transfer` (`InventoryTransfer`: the safeguard and its article), `null` where `storage` is, and `bounda personal-data inventory` prints it, `tenant-keys, in ch, transferred under adequacy (article 45)`; each person's export, whole or streamed, lists in `transfers` (`ExportedTransfer`) the read models with personal data, the master key, the tenant key store and the forget ledger kept elsewhere, with their location, safeguard and article, as article 15(2) asks. On Cloudflare, `durableObjectTenantKeys` and `durableObjectForgetLedger` take a `jurisdiction` of their own with a `safeguard`, and a keeper answers each store's calls only in that store's jurisdiction, so a tenant whose key and ledger are kept apart has a keeper in each; `kvTenantKeys` and `kvForgetLedger` take a `location` together with a `safeguard` (`KvPlacementOptions`), and a KV store that names the objects' jurisdiction is refused, since KV holds to none.
