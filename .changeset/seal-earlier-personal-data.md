A field marked `personal()` or `person()` after events were written no longer leaves them out of `forget`, the export and restriction. The worker walks the stream from its first event and seals in place what each event, and each process lifecycle event's state, holds in the clear of a field marked now, under the key of the person the marks name, who becomes the event's `person`; then the payloads of scheduled commands and dead letters, and the error text of a policy or process letter that failed before the marks and whose work names someone now. The walk keeps its position per fingerprint of the registry's marks, so marks that change walk again and marks that do not never walk twice; past the head it reads what comes after once a minute, so what an instance still on the old marks writes during a deploy is sealed too, the stores included. One instance walks at a time under a lease, and a failure holds the walk for every instance with the same back-off as the retention and the key rotation, all in `bounda_checkpoints`. What a person forgotten before the marks had is sealed under a key no store keeps, so it reads as `null` and they get no key; a value whose event names no person stays in the clear and is logged; a snapshot folded from fields in the clear is dropped. `app.getLag()` reports `personalDataBackfill: { position, caughtUp }` (`PersonalDataBackfillLag`), `null` for an app that marks nothing, each sealed batch is a `bounda.backfill` span, and the worker logs when the walk catches up. Adapters gain `EventStore.reseal`, `Scheduler.reseal` and `DeadLetterStore.reseal` (`ResealedEvent`, `ResealScheduledArgs`, `ResealedDeadLetter`), pinned by the storage contracts.
