What the backfill of personal data left out is closed. A process step's dead letter names the person the state of its instance names when the event names another, as `statePerson`, and seals its text under the keys of both, one inside the other, so `forget`, the export, a restriction and a redaction of either reach it; the backfill finds that person for a letter filed before, by folding its instance up to the failure. A scheduled command records when it was scheduled (`ScheduledCommand.scheduledAt`, `ScheduleArgs.scheduledAt`, `ExportedScheduledCommand.scheduledAt`), and the backfill seals the fields of a person forgotten after that under a key nobody keeps, taking an entry stored before the time was recorded as written before every forgetting. An event of a marked type stored in an older shape is rewritten in its current one, opened, upcast and sealed under the marks of today, at the current schema version (`ResealedEvent.schemaVersion`); while an instance on other marks renews its lease the backfill leaves the shape and walks back once it stops, and `caughtUp` waits for it. A remembered answer names the marks it was sealed under: one sealed under other marks rejects a replay with `IdempotentAnswerErasedError` and is erased by the backfill, and an answer an earlier version kept in the clear under the caller's key is erased and filed under the key's HMAC before the first lookup that misses, so the replay no longer looks the key up in the clear. `IdempotencyStore` gains `list` and `erase` and loses `reseal`; storage migration 9 adds `scheduled_at` to the scheduled commands and `state_person` to the dead letters.
